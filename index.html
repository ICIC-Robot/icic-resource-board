      Why are some numbers marked ≥ or estimated? Share your exact usage

      Lustre only lets each person read their own quota, so the board estimates other members from a daily scan of their folder. Folders the group cannot read are not counted. Run this once on HiPerGator to share your exact usage, which refreshes every 3 hours:

      /blue/du.j/jinjiaguo/duj-github-snapshot/report_storage.sh --install

      Stop at any time with --uninstall. Only your total size and file count are shared, never paths.

      Data & definitions

      Public, read-only, cached data. No UF credentials are collected. An independent group dashboard, not an official UF service. Allocation is not utilization.
